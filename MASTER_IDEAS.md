6. Co-Citation Analysis - Find authorities commonly cited together.
7. Authority Neighborhoods - Find authorities with similar citation patterns.

9. Authority Influence Scores - Measure which authorities matter most.

5. Citation Gravity - Measure how central an authority is to the reasoning.
6. Citation Salience Scoring - Estimate how important a citation is.
7. Framework vs Substantive Detection - Separate routine citations from meaningful ones.
8. Citation Heat Maps - Visualize where citations appear in decisions.
9. Authority Role Classification - Label citations as framework, analytical, supporting, etc.
10. Outcome Citation Detection - Identify authorities used near the final decision.
