iLit: immigration litigation intelligence

iLit is a research tool for Canadian immigration case law. Search a library of public decisions, read each one in a clean, formatted view with its citations and statutes beside the text, and see how judges, authorities and the Federal Court docket fit together.

It is being built independently, on public decisions only, and it is a working demonstration rather than a finished product.
