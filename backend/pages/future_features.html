How it would work

1. At ingestEach paragraph is turned into a numeric “fingerprint” on the agency's computer and stored with the decision.
2. At searchYour question is turned into a fingerprint on the same computer and compared with the stored ones.
3. Nothing leavesNo question, memo or result is sent to an outside AI service. Every result links to its source paragraph.
