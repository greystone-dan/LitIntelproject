## Daniel input required

The following facts cannot be settled from this checkout and need Daniel's answer or a named accountable owner:

1. What exact environment would the Division use, and where are application, database, tunnel/proxy, logs, backups, and support/administration services hosted and processed?
2. Is there an active external authentication/authorization gate today? What identity, user/device scope, access review, and denial evidence demonstrates that anonymous requests cannot reach protected routes?
3. Which audit events are required, where are they retained, who can read/change them, and what is the approved retention/deletion schedule?
4. Is `/memo-citation-check` intended or approved for internal/privileged memos? What are the approved file handling, size, caching, logging, and deletion requirements?
5. What backup schedule and recovery objectives apply, and when was a restore last tested?
6. Which accessibility standard and official-languages obligations apply, and who accepts the resulting assessment?
7. Which third-party services are enabled in the intended environment, what data leaves for each, and which terms/regions/retention have been approved?
8. Who owns source/software licensing review and dependency vulnerability triage, and what evidence/cadence do they require?

## Readiness gate

On repository evidence alone, this checklist does not establish approval for CBSA restricted or privileged material. The application has an optional access gate, disabled unless configured; its live setting and any external gate are unverified. Hosting, retention, audit, backup, provider approval, and organizational requirements also require confirmation. The responsible department determines applicable requirements and whether the system is suitable for its intended use.
